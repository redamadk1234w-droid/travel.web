Why trekkers return

Reliability is the whole job: the right gear, the right pace and a plan for when weather changes.

  • Licensed local guides: First-aid and altitude trained, born in the valleys they lead.
  • Fair porter care: Weight limits, proper clothing and insurance for every porter.
  • Slow, safe acclimatisation: Rest days built into every high-altitude route.
  • One clear price: Permits, transfers and meals listed up front, with no surprise fees.

Common questions

Do I need trekking experience?

Not for the short treks. For hard treks, we recommend regular hiking and good fitness.

What is included in the price?

Guide, porter, permits, lodging and meals during the trek. International tours include flights and hotel as listed.

When is the best time to trek?

October to November and March to May offer the clearest weather.

Do you help with visas for international tours?

Yes. We guide you through the visa forms and documents. Visa fees are paid separately and you need a passport valid for at least 6 months.

Can you deliver a gift outside Kathmandu?

Yes. Tell us the address in the gift form and we will confirm the delivery time and cost.
